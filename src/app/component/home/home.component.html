Computer Room

The **Computer Room** at Knowledge Inn High School is equipped with the latest computers, internet access, and essential software for students to learn programming, web development, and digital skills that will prepare them for the future.

Chemistry Laboratory

Our **Chemistry Laboratory** is a state-of-the-art facility designed to provide students with hands-on experience in chemical experiments. It is equipped with the latest instruments and materials to foster scientific curiosity and understanding.
