A Brief Introduction to Knowledge Inn High School Dhamthal

Knowledge Inn High School was founded on 12 April, 2006. With 12 teaching classes, over 400 pupils, and a dedicated team of 20 teaching and administrative staff, the school has been shaping futures for nearly two decades.

Officially verified by the Education Department of Punjab, the school operates under registration no: EDO/EDU/REG/1085, dated 02-04-2014.

Our Teaching Philosophy: "Excellence"

Although relatively young, Knowledge Inn High School has achieved remarkable milestones. Over 800 students have excelled academically and personally, demonstrating significant improvements in studies, self-management, and adaptability.

The school’s philosophy nurtures creativity, discipline, and a passion for learning. It fosters an environment where students thrive and develop into well-rounded individuals, capable of adapting to life's challenges and embracing opportunities.

Achievements and Facilities

  • Numerous students have won prizes in district-level competitions, excelling in composition, handwriting, art, and English.
  • Equipped with a computer lab, library, and science laboratory, the school fosters a comprehensive learning experience.
  • A refined and well-maintained campus promotes a peaceful and collaborative environment for students and staff alike.
  • Strong focus on developing personal behavior, critical thinking, and a sense of community in students.

Our Goal: To nurture students into well-rounded individuals who are knowledgeable, creative, and capable of making meaningful contributions to society.
